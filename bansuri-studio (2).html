Questions we often hear.

How long does a reel take?

Most reels are ready in 3 to 5 days after the shoot. Festival rush periods can take a little longer, so book early.

Can you edit videos I shoot myself?

Yes. Send your clips on WhatsApp and we will cut, add music and captions, and return a ready-to-post reel.

Do you come to my shop?

Yes, we shoot at your shop or location anywhere in Pilibhit and nearby towns.

How many changes can I ask for?

Each package includes rounds of changes. Tell us what to fix and we will update the video or page.

Do I need to own a domain for a website?

No. We help you choose and register a web address as part of setup.

How do I pay?

We take an advance to start and the rest on delivery. UPI, cash and bank transfer all work.
